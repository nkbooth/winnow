# Work inventory — EXAMPLE

**This is fictional.** It belongs to Alex Rivera, who does not exist. It shows
the shape of the file and doubles as the test suite's material, so the format
below is known to work rather than merely documented.

Every factual claim a cover letter makes must be traceable to a span in this
file, a resume variant, or the reusable paragraphs. The drafter returns the
span it took each claim from and those spans are checked. So write down what
you actually did, in the words you would use out loud — a claim that is not
here cannot be made.

## Systems and ERP

- One of four people — with the CEO, CFO and VP of Operations — choosing the
  ERP an $80M distributor will run on for the next decade. Ran the vendor
  evaluation, scored the responses against a weighted requirements matrix, and
  wrote the recommendation the board approved.
- Owned four commercial ERP add-on implementations end to end: demand
  forecasting, order management, inventory replenishment, and a customer
  portal. Scoped, selected, configured, migrated data, trained, supported.
- Built a capacity scheduler covering 400 workcenters, replacing a spreadsheet
  that three people maintained by hand. It runs nightly and the shop floor
  reads it every morning.
- In the last year, shipped an MCP server exposing ERP data to the company through Teams, gated
  by role and permission inherited from the ERP's own security groups.

## Data and reporting

- Defined the daily KPI report with the executive team — revenue invoiced,
  top-performing items, profitability by location, new orders booked. It is
  their standing morning read.
- Built ETL pipelines across the ERP, its add-ons, and two storefronts, and
  moved the reporting cache to managed Postgres, cutting BI report processing
  time by roughly 70%.
- Built dashboards measuring rep and territory performance: outreach activity,
  sales and margin, and same-customer growth trend.

## Leadership

- Led a 12-person inside sales team and defined its operating metrics — call
  volume and duration, coverage, quote win rate, order volume.
- Four consecutive years of 12% growth at flat headcount, achieved by
  developing the team rather than adding to it.
- Hired, coached and promoted three people into roles above the one they were
  hired for. The measure of the work is whether the team needs me less each
  quarter.

## What is not here

Deliberately. These are the boundaries a draft must not cross:

- No people-management above 12 direct reports.
- No P&L ownership.
- No experience in regulated finance, healthcare, or public sector.
- No formal qualification in computer science.
